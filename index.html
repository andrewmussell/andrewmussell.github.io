Andrew Mussell is a Hardware Engineer with a degree in Electrical Engineering and Computer Science (Minor in Bioengineering) from the University of California, Berkeley. He participates in Formula Electric at Berkeley, IEEE, and the Pilawa Power Electronics Research Group.

Portfolio Recent Work

A selection of hardware engineering projects spanning embedded systems, power electronics, and consumer electronics.

Custom 20kW Inverter

In collaboration with Pilawa Power Electronics Research Group and Formula Electric at Berkeley, designed a custom 20kW Inverter to drive a dyno testbench and electric racecar.

Embedded Smart Watch

Built a smart watch with a custom PCB and firmware — including a real-time clock, WiFi, accelerometer, pressure, temperature, ambient light sensor, and battery gauge IC with integrated USB-C charging. Features fall detection, cover-to-sleep, and WiFi time sync on boot (ESP32).

STM32 MicroBasic Block

In collaboration with Formula Electric at Berkeley, designed a Microcontroller Daughterboard for embedded control in electric motorsport applications.
